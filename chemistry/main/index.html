How to play

  • Look in the bottom guide strip for your next clue.
  • Tap a sample station to compare the monitor, bottle and paper request.
  • For moving a bottle, drag it to the highlighted rack. You can also select it, then select the rack.
  • At the centrifuge, place tubes directly opposite one another. Cap colour does not decide which tubes balance.
  • Close the lid before Spin. After it stops, open the lid and retrieve Ian's labelled grey-top sample.
  • Keyboard: use Tab to move, then Enter or Space to choose.
